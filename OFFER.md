**Find out exactly which ads are wasting your money, in 48 hours.**

## FAQ
**Do you need access to my ads account?** No. A spreadsheet export is all we need.

**What happens to my data?** It's used only to prepare your report and is deleted once the report is delivered. We never share it.

**What counts as "avoidable spend"?** Spend on ads with zero conversions, plus spend on ads costing more than 1.5× your target cost per conversion, above what their conversions were worth at your target. The report shows the calculation line by line.

**What if my account is already well run?** Then you'll have independent confirmation — and if we find less than £99 of avoidable spend, you get your money back.

**Is this a guarantee of results?** No. It's an analysis of your past data with clear recommendations. Results after you act on them depend on your market, offer and creative.
