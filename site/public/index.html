Export guide

How to export your data

We only need a spreadsheet. Never send us your password, and you don't need to give us access to your account.

Meta (Facebook and Instagram) — Ads Manager
  1. Open Ads Manager at adsmanager.facebook.com.
  2. Click the Ads tab (not Campaigns or Ad sets).
  3. Set the date range, top right, to Last 30 days.
  4. Check these columns are showing: Amount spent, Impressions, Link clicks, Results.
  5. Click Reports (or the export icon) → Export table data → .csv.
Google Ads
  1. Open Google Ads at ads.google.com.
  2. In the left menu, go to Campaigns → Ads.
  3. Set the date range, top right, to Last 30 days.
  4. Check these columns are showing: Cost, Impr., Clicks, Conversions.
  5. Click the download icon above the table and choose .csv.

Menu names vary slightly between accounts. If you get stuck, email us a screenshot and we'll point you to it.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do you need access to my ads account?

No. A spreadsheet export is all we need. We never ask for passwords or account access, and we make no changes to your account.

What counts as "avoidable spend"?

Spend on ads that produced no conversions, plus, for ads costing more than 1.5 times your target cost per conversion, the spend above what their conversions were worth at your target. The report shows the calculation for every ad.

What happens to my data?

It is used only to prepare your report and is deleted once the report is delivered. Founding clients' data is kept until the 30-day re-audit unless they ask us to delete it sooner. We never share it.

What if my account is already well run?

Then you have independent confirmation. If we find less than £99 of avoidable spend, you get a full refund.

Is this a guarantee of future results?

No. It is an analysis of your past performance with clear recommendations. What happens after you act depends on your market, offer and creative.
