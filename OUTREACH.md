# Outreach — first 10 founding clients

**Rules:** write to one named person at a time. Mention something real about their business. Never claim results you don't have. Always offer an easy opt-out.
UK law (PECR): unsolicited marketing email is OK to limited companies and LLPs if you identify yourself and offer an opt-out. Sole traders and partnerships need prior consent, so use LinkedIn or a call for them.

## Who to contact
- UK businesses visibly running ads: check the **Meta Ad Library** (several active ads for 30+ days) or see them in Google search ads for their keywords.
- Ecommerce brands, clinics, trades and home services, gyms, local services with online booking.
- Decision maker: founder, owner, marketing manager, or head of growth.

## Email (cold)

**Subject:** Quick question about your {Meta/Google} ads

Hi {First name},

I noticed {Company} has been running {e.g. "several ads for your winter range on Instagram"} for a while.

I run a short audit for businesses spending £1,000+ a month on ads: you send me a spreadsheet export (no account access), and within 48 hours I show you exactly which ads are wasting money, what to pause, and where to move the budget.

It's £99, and if I don't find at least £99 of avoidable spend, you get a full refund.

I'm taking on 10 founding clients, who also get a free re-audit 30 days later. Here's a sample report so you can see what you'd get: {sample link}

Worth a look?

{Your name}
Crewlet · {website}

*If this isn't relevant, just reply "no thanks" and I won't contact you again.*

{Your name}

## Reply handling
- **"How do I export?"** → send the 2-minute export guide (Meta: Ads Manager → Reports → Export table at ad level, last 30 days; Google Ads: Ads → Download → CSV).
- **"Is my data safe?"** → used only for the report, deleted after delivery, never shared.
- **"Can you just fix it?"** → "The audit comes first. If you'd like help implementing the changes afterwards, we can talk about that."
- **"Too expensive"** → "Understood. It's refunded if I find less than £99 of waste, so the risk is on me."

## Tracking
Log every contact (company, person, channel, date, reply, outcome). Target: 50 personalised messages → 10 conversations → 3–5 paid audits in the first two weeks. These are planning assumptions, not benchmarks.
