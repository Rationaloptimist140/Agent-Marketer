# How to send us your ads data (2 minutes)

We only need a spreadsheet export. **Never send us your password or give us account access.**

**What we need:** the last 30 days, at **ad** level, with spend, impressions, clicks and conversions (Meta calls them "Results").

---

## Meta (Facebook & Instagram) — Ads Manager

1. Open **Ads Manager** at adsmanager.facebook.com.
2. Click the **Ads** tab (not Campaigns or Ad sets).
3. Set the date range (top right) to **Last 30 days**.
4. Make sure these columns are showing: **Amount spent, Impressions, Link clicks, Results**. The default "Performance" columns usually include them.
5. Click **Reports** (or the export icon) → **Export table data**.
6. Choose **.csv** and click **Export**.
7. Email us the file.

## Google Ads

1. Open **Google Ads** at ads.google.com.
2. In the left menu, go to **Campaigns → Ads** (sometimes shown as "Ads & assets").
3. Set the date range (top right) to **Last 30 days**.
4. Make sure these columns are showing: **Cost, Impr., Clicks, Conversions**.
5. Click the **download icon** above the table and choose **.csv**.
6. Email us the file.

Menu names can vary slightly between accounts. If you can't find something, reply with a screenshot and we'll point you to it.

---

## Also tell us (one line each)

- **What a conversion is worth to you**, e.g. "an average order is £45" or "a new customer is worth about £300". We use this to set your target cost per conversion.
- **Anything we should know**, e.g. "the brand campaign is intentional" or "we changed the website on the 12th".

## What happens to your data

We use it only to prepare your report and delete it once the report is delivered (founding clients: we keep it until the 30-day re-audit, unless you ask us to delete it sooner). We never share it.
